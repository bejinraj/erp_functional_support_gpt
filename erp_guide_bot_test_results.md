1. ## Purpose

To formally request goods or services before a Purchase Order can be created.

## Steps

1. Navigate to Materials Management → Purchase Requisition → Create.

2. Enter the material or service description, quantity, and required delivery date.

3. Select the appropriate plant, storage location, and purchasing group, as applicable.

4. Enter the estimated price and account assignment, such as cost center or GL account, if required.

5. Attach supporting documents or specifications, if applicable.

6. Review all entered details for accuracy.

7. Submit the Purchase Requisition for approval.

8. Note the PR number for future reference.

## Common Issues

- **Role Clarification:** This process is normally performed by a Requester or Department Employee. If you do not have access to create a PR, check with your supervisor to ensure you have the correct system role assigned.

- **Missing Account Assignment:** If the system blocks your submission due to missing account assignment details (such as a cost center or GL account), consult your department manager or finance team for the correct codes.

4. ## Purpose

To ensure that Purchase Orders comply with company policies and are properly authorized before issuance.

## Steps

1. Navigate to the Purchase Order Approval or Release section.
2. Search for the PO using its document number.
3. Review supplier details, order value, quantities, prices, and delivery terms.
4. Compare the PO with the approved PR, quotation, or contract.
5. Verify compliance with the applicable approval limits.
6. Approve or release the PO if all details are correct.
7. Return the PO for correction if discrepancies are identified.
8. Confirm that the PO has reached the approved or released status.

## Common Issues

- **Role Clarification:** This process is normally performed by a Procurement Manager or Authorized Approver. If you are setting up or modifying the underlying workflow rules rather than approving a document, system configuration rights are required.
- **System Configuration Access:** Setting up new approval workflow paths or release strategies requires administrative or system configuration access. If you do not have authorization to configure workflows, please submit a request to your IT or ERP administration team.
